Bokoboy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Revised: October 15, 2026

onigiriboys (the "developer"), the developer of Bokoboy (the "app"), respects the privacy of everyone who uses the app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information or other data. No data is sent to the developer's servers or to any third party. The app does not use the network and requires no account.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your records, the app saves the following only on your device. It is never sent off the device.

  • Your team (chosen allies) and settings (language, volume, swipe sensitivity, slash style)
  • Your best depth with its date and team, number of rounds played, total depth reached, and unlocked allies
  • Whether you have played the practice round
  • The app version and date when the app last asked you to rate it on the App Store (so it does not ask again in the same version)

This information is deleted when you delete the app. Records and settings can also be erased from "Settings > Reset records and settings" in the app. It may be included in device backups (such as iCloud), which are handled by Apple and your own settings.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party services. It does not track you. When the app asks you to rate it, it uses Apple's built-in rating prompt. Ratings and reviews go to the App Store and are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

4. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app's features change. Any change will be posted on this page with its revision date. If the handling of data changes significantly, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please email onigiriboysgames@gmail.com (see also the support page).
