Bokoboy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Revised: October 28, 2026 (added the ranking to the browser version)

onigiriboys (the "developer"), the developer of Bokoboy (the iPhone app and the browser version playable at onigiriboys.com; together the "app"), respects the privacy of everyone who uses the app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information collected for the ranking

If you join the app's ranking, the app sends only the following to the developer's server (run on Cloudflare's service) and stores it there. If you don't join the ranking, nothing is sent.

  • The name you enter for the ranking
  • Your records: depth, the date you played, and the team (allies) you used
  • Random numbers made by the app (a number and passcode for each player who joins, and a number for each device used to report names). They are not linked to your device information or Apple ID

Your name and records are shown to other players on the ranking, so please don't use your real name or contact details. They are used only to show the ranking and to deal with inappropriate names. They are never used for ads or tracking, and never sold or given to third parties. Network information such as your IP address is not stored as part of your records (Cloudflare handles it while delivering requests).

Viewing the ranking also uses the network, but if you haven't joined, nothing that identifies you is sent. The iPhone app and the browser version share the same ranking, handled the same way (a browser player appears as a separate player from their iPhone app). Records from rounds played with the keyboard are not sent.

You can delete your name and records from the server at any time in the app: Ranking > My Best > Delete my ranking data. Inappropriate names can be reported from the ranking screen. Names with enough reports are hidden automatically, then reviewed by the developer, who either restores the name or removes the player from the ranking.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your records, the app saves the following only on your device (in the browser version, only in that browser). Apart from the ranking in section 1, it is never sent off the device.

  • Your team (chosen allies) and settings (language, volume, swipe sensitivity, slash style)
  • Your best depth with its date and team, number of rounds played, total depth reached, and unlocked allies
  • Whether you have played the practice round
  • The app version and date when the app last asked you to rate it on the App Store (so it does not ask again in the same version)
  • Your ranking name, number and passcode, records not sent yet, and players you hid on your screen

This information is deleted when you delete the app (for the browser version, when you clear the site data for onigiriboys.com in your browser). To delete your name and records on the ranking server, first use the method in section 1: without the passcode saved on your device, you can no longer delete them yourself. Records and settings can also be erased from "Settings > Reset records and settings" in the app. It may be included in device backups (such as iCloud), which are handled by Apple and your own settings.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads and no analytics tools. It does not track you. The only outside service is the ranking server (Cloudflare). The browser version's pages are also delivered by Cloudflare (with visitor counting turned off). When the app asks you to rate it, it uses Apple's built-in rating prompt. Ratings and reviews go to the App Store and are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

4. Children

Apart from the ranking name and records, the app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Please remind children not to put their real name, school or contact details in their ranking name.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app's features change. Any change will be posted on this page with its revision date. If the handling of data changes significantly, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please email onigiriboysgames@gmail.com (see also the support page).
