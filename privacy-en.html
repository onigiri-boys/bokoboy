Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Revised: November 20, 2026 (added Google Analytics for users who agree; usage analytics and cumulative digging distance were added on November 18)

onigiriboys (the "developer"), the developer of Bokoboy (the iPhone app and the browser version playable at onigiriboys.com; together the "app"), respects the privacy of everyone who uses the app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Information collected for the ranking

If you join the app's ranking, the app sends the following information to the developer's server (run on Cloudflare's service) and stores it there. Joining is optional. Usage analytics are described separately in section 3.

  • The name you enter for the ranking
  • Your records: depth, the date you played, and the team (allies) you used
  • Random numbers made by the app (a number and passcode for each player who joins, and a number for each device used to report names). They are not linked to your device information or Apple ID

Your name and records are shown to other players on the ranking, so please don't use your real name or contact details. They are used only to show the ranking and to deal with inappropriate names. They are never used for ads or tracking, and never sold or given to third parties. Network information such as your IP address is not stored as part of your records (Cloudflare handles it while delivering requests).

Viewing the ranking also uses the network, but if you haven't joined, no ranking player identifier is sent. The iPhone app and the browser version share the same ranking, handled the same way (a browser player appears as a separate player from their iPhone app). Records from rounds played with the keyboard are not sent to the ranking.

You can delete your name and records from the server at any time in the app: Ranking > My Best > Delete my ranking data. Inappropriate names can be reported from the ranking screen. Names with enough reports are hidden automatically, then reviewed by the developer, who either restores the name or removes the player from the ranking.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep your records, the app saves the following on your device (in the browser version, in that browser). The information described in sections 1 and 3 is also sent to the server.

  • Your team (chosen allies) and settings (language, volume, swipe sensitivity, slash style)
  • Your best depth with its date and team, number of rounds played, total depth reached, and unlocked allies
  • Whether you have played the practice round
  • The app version and date when the app last asked you to rate it on the App Store (so it does not ask again in the same version)
  • Your ranking name, number and passcode, records not sent yet, and players you hid on your screen
  • Whether to send usage data, a random analytics identifier, and usage data not sent yet
  • Your choice to agree to or decline Google Analytics. If you agree, Google's analytics identifier and temporary analytics data (cookies in the browser version)

This information is deleted when you delete the app (for the browser version, when you clear the site data for onigiriboys.com in your browser). To delete your name and records on the ranking server, first use the method in section 1: without the passcode saved on your device, you can no longer delete them yourself. "Settings > Reset records and settings" erases your records and settings but keeps your ranking identity and choices about sending usage data and Google Analytics. Data may be included in device backups (such as iCloud), which are handled by Apple and your own settings.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app has no ads. The usage data below is used to improve the game, not for ads or advertising-related tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

3.1. Analytics on the developer's server

To improve the game and make practice easier to understand, the iPhone app and the browser version on onigiriboys.com send the following usage data to the developer's server (Cloudflare). This is separate from joining the ranking.

  • Session starts and ends, play and practice starts and ends, success, timeout or leaving a round, practice steps, and ally or slash-style unlocks
  • The time, duration, depth, combo and attack counts, wall hits, peak height, personal-best updates, the team used, and whether a keyboard was used
  • The app version, whether you use the iPhone or browser version, and the UI language
  • A random analytics identifier generated by the app and identifiers for each session and round. These group usage from the same installation. They are not linked to your ranking name or identifiers, or to your Apple ID

This developer-server analytics service does not receive your name, contact details, advertising identifier, device model, location, page URLs or swipe coordinates. IP addresses and browser information are not stored in its analytics records (Cloudflare handles them while delivering requests). Individual analytics records are not shown to other players and are removed after 90 days by an hourly cleanup, which may take up to about one additional hour. They are not sold.

To understand the distance dug across the game, we sum the depths and counts of successful rounds received. These totals retain only the platform (iPhone or browser), play or practice mode, whether a keyboard was used, and the first and last successful round times. They contain no personal, installation, session or round identifiers, and no teams. These all-time aggregates without personal identifiers are kept after the individual records are deleted at 90 days. Regular play, practice and rounds using a keyboard are totaled separately.

You can stop sending data at any time in Settings > About > Send usage data. Turning it off deletes unsent usage data and the analytics identifier on your device. Turning it on again creates a new identifier. This setting does not delete individual records already received; they are deleted once they are more than 90 days old. Amounts already added to the all-time aggregates are not subtracted. You can play the same game without sending usage data. The YouTube Playables version does not send to this analytics service.

3.2. Google Analytics for users who agree

Versions that support Google Analytics also use Google LLC's service to measure how the game is used (Firebase Analytics on iPhone and the Google tag in the browser version). This applies only if you choose "Agree" on the explanation screen or turn on "Settings > About > Use Google Analytics". It starts off, and an earlier choice to send usage data is not treated as consent to Google Analytics. You can play the same game without agreeing.

While you have agreed and "Send usage data" is also on, the following information is sent to Google. Your ranking name, identifier and passcode, and the developer-server analytics identifier, are not sent to Google.

  • Play and practice starts, ends and results, practice steps, ally and slash-style unlocks, depth, combos, attacks, wall hits, peak height, duration, personal-best updates, the team used, whether a keyboard was used, and the app version
  • Automatically measured events such as first app opens, updates and session starts, engagement time, and information such as device category, operating system, browser and language
  • Approximate location such as country and region inferred from IP addresses. The device's location services, such as GPS, are not used. Google Analytics uses IP addresses to estimate location, then discards them before logging the data
  • Random app-installation or browser identifiers generated by Google to group usage from the same installation or browser. This is not completely anonymous information
  • The fixed URL of the browser game page, the referring site when arriving from an external site, and allowed referral categories such as X or YouTube. URL search terms, arbitrary entered values and the path of the referring page are not sent

Names, contact details and swipe coordinates are not sent to Google Analytics. The iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA) and identifier for vendor (IDFV) are not collected. Google signals, advertising personalization and advertising-service integrations are not used.

In the browser version, Google measurement loads only after you agree and stores cookies specific to the game page. No measurement, including cookieless measurement, is sent before you agree or after sending is turned off. Cookies last up to 90 days and may be renewed when you return. Your browser may remove them sooner.

Google's user-level and event-level data is managed with a two-month retention setting, without extending that period when you return. Google deletes expired data during its regular processing. This retention setting does not apply to Google's standard aggregated reports. It is separate from the developer-server service's 90-day period and its all-time totals without personal identifiers described in section 3.1.

You can withdraw consent in Settings > About > Use Google Analytics. Turning off "Send usage data" also stops sending to Google. On iPhone, this clears Google's local analytics data and app-installation identifier. In the browser version, it clears unsent information and the game's analytics cookies. This does not delete data Google has already received or its aggregated reports. Your choices are kept when you reset records and settings. The YouTube Playables version does not use Google Analytics.

For Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google may process information on servers outside your country.

3.3. Other services

The browser version's pages are also delivered by Cloudflare, but Cloudflare Web Analytics visitor counting is not used. When the app asks you to rate it, it uses Apple's built-in rating prompt. Ratings and reviews go to the App Store and are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

4. Children

Information from all users, including children, is handled as described in sections 1 and 3. Please remind children not to put their real name, school or contact details in their ranking name. Usage data can be turned off using the setting in section 3.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app's features change. Any change will be posted on this page with its revision date. If the handling of data changes significantly, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.

6. Contact
