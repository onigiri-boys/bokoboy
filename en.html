Bokoboy

Beat it up. Smash it deep.

日本語

Bokoboy is a portrait 3D action game for iPhone, played with simple swipes. Chain your clay allies' attacks to launch the enemy sky-high, then smash it deep underground with one final blow. The depth you reach is your score.

How to play

  1. Swipe left or right across the enemy. An ally appears and launches it. Each round lasts 10 seconds from your first swipe.
  2. Alternate sides, then go up. Chaining allies makes the up icon glow. When it glows, swipe up for a big launch.
  3. Watch the spiked walls. Hitting from the same side again and again drifts the enemy into the spikes and costs height.
  4. Swipe down to smash. Within 10 seconds. Straight down through the center of the enemy goes deepest.

Your first "Play" starts with a practice round where time stands still. You can read the guide or practice again any time from "Help" on the main menu.

FAQ

How do I get new allies?

They unlock one by one based on how many rounds you play, your total depth and your best depth. Locked allies show their condition and your progress on the Team screen.

How do I erase my records?

Tap "Settings > Reset records and settings" twice. Your records, team and settings return to the start. This cannot be undone.

What happens to my records on a new iPhone?

Records are saved only on your device. They may carry over if you restore from a device backup, but no other transfer method is supported.

There is no sound

Sound is muted while the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent. Please also check "Settings > Volume" in the game.

Language

The game starts in Japanese on a Japanese iPhone and in English otherwise. You can change it at the top of Settings ("言語 / Language").

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 15.0 or later. No network connection is used.
